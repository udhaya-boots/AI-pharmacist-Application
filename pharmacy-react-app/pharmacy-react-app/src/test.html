Kape Kapi & Exports

Kape Kapi & Exports is a new company in the coffee export market with 15 years of experience in the coffee industry. The domestic market company is Coorg Commodities & Coffee Curers.

  • Well-maintained coffee curing facility in Kushalnagar, Karnataka.
  • Sourcing raw coffee directly from farmers to avoid middlemen.
  • Conducting certification processes for farmers including Organic, Rainforest Alliance, 4C, Fairtrade, etc.
  • Certified volume of 5000 Mt of cleaned coffee.
  • Complete traceability from farm to cup.
  • Supplying to major MNCs in the coffee industry including Nestle, ITC, Olam, NKG, Ecom, LDC, Allana Sons, etc.
  • Markets covered: Europe, Middle East, Africa.
  • Capability to supply coffee throughout the year.
